HOW TO CAPTURE A DEBUG LOG
==========================

If you have a problem and need to send a log to support, use one
of the two methods below. The receiver does NOT create log files
automatically — you must capture one on purpose.


METHOD 1: USB TRIGGER (Recommended)
-------------------------------------
What you need: a USB drive (any size, FAT32 or ext4).

Step 1 - Create a trigger file on the USB drive named exactly:

          ENIGMA2_DEBUG.trigger

         (no extension, no spaces, name must be exact)

Step 2 - Insert the USB drive into your receiver.

Step 3 - Reboot the receiver.
         The trigger is only detected at startup.

Step 4 - Reproduce the problem.
         Use the receiver normally until the issue occurs.
         Note: the receiver may feel slightly slower than usual
         while debug logging is active. This is normal.

Step 5 - Reboot the receiver again to flush the log to disk.

Step 6 - Remove the USB drive.
         Open the folder  tnap-debug/  on the USB drive.
         Send ALL files in that folder to admin@tnapimages.com.

IMPORTANT: Remove or rename the trigger file after you are done.
If you leave it on the USB, debug mode will activate every time
you insert that drive.


METHOD 2: SSH OR TELNET SESSION
---------------------------------
Use this if you do not have a USB drive available, or if you
want to watch the output live on your computer.

Requirements: SSH or Telnet access to the receiver.

Step 1 - Open an SSH or Telnet connection to the receiver.

Step 2 - Stop enigma2:

          init 4

Step 3 - Start enigma2 and capture output to a file:

          enigma2 2>&1 | tee /home/root/logs/enigma2_manual.log

Step 4 - The receiver screen will come back on.
         Reproduce the problem.

Step 5 - Reboot the receiver when done. Before rebooting, copy
         the log file off the receiver — it will be deleted on
         the next boot.

Step 6 - Send the log file to admin@tnapimages.com.

IMPORTANT: Keep the SSH/Telnet window open the whole time.
Closing it will stop the log capture.


WHAT TO INCLUDE IN YOUR EMAIL
-------------------------------
  - Your receiver model
  - Image version  (Menu > Information > About)
  - What you were doing when the problem occurred
  - The log file(s)
